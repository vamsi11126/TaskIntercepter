TaskIntercept catches you at the exact moment you open Instagram or YouTube,
and shows you what you planned to do first — before the app's own interface
even loads.

Nothing is blocked. It just makes an automatic habit into a conscious choice
again.

FEATURES

* Set what you're planning to do, with a priority (High / Medium / Low) —
  track more than one thing at a time
* Opening a monitored app shows a brief overlay with your top task(s) before
  the app itself is visible
* A 10-second pacing countdown — nothing forced happens at zero, you always
  make the real choice
* "Return to Task" sends you home, or "Continue Anyway" lets the app
  through — no shame, no lockout
* Choose which apps get monitored (Instagram and YouTube by default),
  adjust the overlay's duration, or pause interception entirely with a
  master switch

PERMISSIONS, HONESTLY

* Accessibility Service: the only way for an Android app to detect when
  another app opens, before its UI loads. TaskIntercept reads window-state
  change events only — which app just came to the foreground — and never
  reads screen content.
* Display over other apps: needed to draw the brief overlay on top of the
  app you just opened.
* Ignore battery optimizations (optional): some manufacturers aggressively
  kill background accessibility services; this exemption helps
  TaskIntercept keep working reliably.

All task data stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, ever. No
analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Full detail in the app's PRIVACY.md.
